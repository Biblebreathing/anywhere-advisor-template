## Patterns
- **What everyone says (the crowded messages to avoid):** "education, not a sales pitch"; "make your savings last"; "reduce risk without sacrificing returns"; generic Roth/IRMAA/QCD checklists; free steak dinners.
- **What nobody says (an open position):** No local competitor found speaks to **engineers retiring from specific local tech/manufacturing employers with RSUs** *and* rental property *and* a business, in one plan. No one found pairs **Colorado-specific estate mechanics** (beneficiary deeds, durable-POA default, probate minimums) with tax and income planning in a single webinar.
- **Offers nobody makes:** No local firm found offers a **numbers-first self-assessment** (a "Tax Trap score" or "retirement paycheck readiness" score) for engineers. No local landlord-exit program was found ("tenants and toilets"). No local advisor book on Colorado retirement was found. No local *webinar* series (as opposed to in-person events) was found except Mercer's national content.

What is distinctive is Greg's **local tax-prep firm + estate documents + RIA under one owner, with his own landlord and business-owner experience**.
