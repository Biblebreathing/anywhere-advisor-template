## What this means for the advisor `[INFERRED]`
1. **The 65+ market is growing fast and owns its homes.** Larimer's 65+ population grew about half again in ten years [F-073], and 82% of 65+ households own their homes [F-077], worth a median $610K [F-076]. That supports an **Estate** track built on Colorado-specific tools (beneficiary deeds, probate minimums, durable POAs) rather than estate *tax*, which almost no prospect faces [F-038, F-040].
2. **The pre-retiree income spike is the Tax Trap audience.** Householders 45–64 have a median income of $141K, about double the 65+ median [F-075]. The drop at retirement is the conversion and IRMAA window [F-021, F-024].
3. **Colorado's retirement-income tax break is a hook, but not for Greg's high earners before 65.** At $200K–$300K of income, the 55–64 Social Security break is lost (AGI test) and only the $20K cap applies [F-035]. At 65+ the full SS subtraction returns [F-034]. That's a concrete "turn 65 in Colorado" talking point.
4. **The tech employer base is real but name-check carefully.** Woodward, HPE and Broadcom are documented [F-079]; Intel, AMD and HP Inc. aren't verified as current [F-082–F-084].
5. **LTC costs are high locally** (Colorado in-home care is 7th-highest in the U.S. [F-085]). That's material for a later LTC track and for the "protect the surviving spouse" message.
