# Research Summary — Greg W. Anderson

One page. The 10 most useful findings for building the offer, webinars, website and book. Each cites a fact ID in `fact-registry.md` (all accessed 2026-10-19).

1. **RMDs start at 75 for anyone born in 1960 or later** (73 for older clients). Most of Greg's 60–66-year-old prospects get a longer, plannable window before forced withdrawals [F-013, F-014]. *Use it for:* Tax Trap webinar opener; book chapter on the IRA tax bill.
2. **Medicare IRMAA uses income from two years back**, and 2026 Part B runs from $202.90 to $689.90 a month per person (plus Part D surcharges). A conversion or property sale at 63 shows up in premiums at 65 [F-020–F-022, F-024]. *Use it for:* Tax Trap "hidden cost" slide; ad headline.
3. **RSUs are wage income when the shares are delivered** (fair market value at vesting/settlement); only later gains are capital gains [F-025]. Verified local employers with large technical workforces: Woodward (HQ), HPE, Broadcom [F-079–F-081]. *Use it for:* Tax Trap RSU segment; LinkedIn ads to local engineers (don't name employers in copy).
4. **Colorado's retirement tax break is real but age- and income-tested.** At 65+ all taxable Social Security plus up to $24K per person of pension/IRA income comes off state income; at 55–64 the full SS break requires AGI ≤ $75K/$95K, which Greg's clients won't meet [F-032–F-035]. State rate 4.4% (2025) [F-031]. *Use it for:* "Turning 65 in Colorado" webinar segment; website tax page.
5. **Colorado has no estate or inheritance tax, and the federal exemption is $15M per person (2026).** The estate pitch should be about incapacity and court time, not tax [F-038, F-040]. *Use it for:* Estate funnel myth-buster; book Estate chapter.
6. **Colorado probate has built-in waiting periods:** a 4-month creditor window, a one-year outside bar, and no informal closing until 6 months after appointment. The small-estate affidavit stops at $88,000 (2026), versus a $610,000 median Larimer home [F-042, F-044–F-046, F-076]. *Use it for:* Estate funnel opt-in page; "One Year and One Day" story setup.
7. **Colorado tools families don't know:** beneficiary deeds (house passes at death, owner keeps control, a will can't override) and durable-by-default POAs [F-048–F-052]. *Use it for:* Estate review checklist; landlord-exit track.
8. **Most adults have no will:** 24% have one (Caring.com 2025); 53% of 50–64-year-olds (Gallup 2021) [F-053, F-054]. *Use it for:* Estate ad hooks; webinar opener (state survey and year).
9. **Larimer's 65+ population grew about 52% in ten years** to 68,374 (18.3%, above Colorado's 16.5%), and 82% own their homes [F-072, F-073, F-077]. 45–64 householders earn a median $141K, about double the 65+ median [F-075]. *Use it for:* Local market slide; website "Why Northern Colorado" copy.
10. **Retirement arrives early and confidence is falling:** retirees' median retirement age is 62 and nearly half retired earlier than planned; worker confidence is 61%, the lowest since 2017 [F-065, F-066]. Life expectancy at 65 is 19.7 years (women 20.8) [F-055]. *Use it for:* Retirement Paycheck webinar; book introduction.
