## Compliance notes
- **"Free" estate documents:** the website offers free POAs and wills. In Anywhere Advisor estate funnels the program line is exact: *advisors are paid to fund trusts, never to sell or create them.* Decide with Greg's approver how the estate funnel describes documents (who prepares them, what's free, what isn't) before any ad runs.
- **The CPA What-If review** is a tax service of Fort Collins Tax Advisory Group. Keep tax advice and investment marketing disclosures separate as the approver requires.
- **No guarantee language** about income, principal or taxes. "Principal-protected" appears only with conditions and only in approved annuity materials.
- **Reviews and testimonials** (Google, Paul and Rhonda, ThreeBestRated) need Marketing Rule handling before use in ads or webinars.
- **"Family Office Membership"** is a working name; "family office" may overstate the services. Get the approver's OK or rename.
