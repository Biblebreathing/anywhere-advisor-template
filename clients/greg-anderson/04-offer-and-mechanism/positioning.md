## The one-liner
"I help engineers and long-time employees in their 60s turn what they've saved into a retirement paycheck that lasts for both spouses, with the taxes and the family documents handled in the same office, using the PATH Plan, so they're not guessing and nothing falls between three different offices."

## The 30-second version
"Most people I meet saved well. They just saved in accounts built to grow, not to pay them, and their advisor, their CPA and their attorney have never been in the same room. I run one office that does all three. We start with your paycheck: what you need and where it comes from. Then we position your assets so a bad year early in retirement doesn't derail it. Then we look at your tax return with our CPA and map out Roth conversions and RMDs. Then we get your trust, wills and powers of attorney done. We call it the PATH Plan. Clients tell me the relief is having a path to run on."

## The tension: who to lead with (recommendation)
- **Lead with the engineer and long-time employee** ($1M–$4M) for the first 90 days. They are his largest natural market, the client he enjoys most, and the fastest route to appointment volume through the estate funnel and the Tax Trap webinar.
- **Build the upmarket door second** (months 4–9): a landlord-exit campaign ("tenants and toilets") and a business-sale planning campaign. These bring the $5M–$10M households.
- **The same mechanism serves both.** PATH works for a $1.5M engineer and for a $7M landlord; only the entry webinar changes.
