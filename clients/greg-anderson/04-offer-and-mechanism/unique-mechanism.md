## 3. The steps (The PATH Plan)
Four steps in the order Greg actually works **[INFERRED from KB §5; Greg must confirm in the review]**. The annual review is the loop, not a fifth letter: "we walk your PATH again every year."

| # | Step | What happens | The client's question | Deliverable | Webinar track | Assessment section | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paycheck** | Find what the couple needs each year and where it will come from: Social Security timing, pensions, portfolio, rentals, part-time work | "Will our income last for both of us?" | **Retirement Paycheck Map** (one page: income by source, by year, and the gap) | Retirement Paycheck | Income readiness | "Recreating $200K to $300K of annual income that lasts for both spouses"; Social Security teacher |
| 2 | **Assets** | Position the money by job: income money out of full market risk, growth money for later, reserves for LTC and emergencies. Uses his risk score tool. | "What happens if the market drops the year we retire?" | **Risk & Reserve Report** (risk score + allocation by purpose) | Asset Protection + LTC | Risk and protection | Story 1; "Get Your Risk Score"; RICP sequence risk |
| 3 | **Taxes** | The in-house CPA reviews last year's return and maps the years ahead: Roth conversions, RMDs, RSUs, rental and business sales | "How much of this is really ours after taxes?" | **CPA What-If Tax Timeline** (from his existing "CPA What-If review of your last tax return") | Tax ("Tax Trap") | Tax exposure | Fort Collins Tax Advisory Group; Roth conversions; RSUs |
| 4 | **Heirs** | (Colorado has no estate or inheritance tax and the federal exemption is $15M (F-038), so sell incapacity protection and avoiding court time, not tax: the $88K small-estate cap (F-042) vs. a $610K median Larimer home.) Put the documents in place and check every beneficiary: living trust, wills, POAs, special-needs and blended-family planning; store them in the client vault | "If one of us dies tomorrow, is the other one OK?" | **Family Legacy Binder** (documents + beneficiary audit + vault access) | Estate | Legacy readiness | Story 2; free POAs/wills (website); generationalvault.com |
| ↻ | **Walk the PATH every year** | Annual review of all four steps with tax, estate and investments at one table | "Are we still on track?" | Updated PATH Map | — | — | KB §9 "360 degrees" |

**Compliance notes on the steps:** "Assets" must never imply principal protection as a promise; annuity features are described with their conditions.
