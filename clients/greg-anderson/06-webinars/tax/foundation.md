## 2. The Through Line

> **Wrong belief:** "I'll be in a lower bracket when I retire, and my CPA handles taxes every April."
> **True belief:** "Retirement taxes are planned in decades, not filed in April. The years before my RMDs start are the window, and they don't come back."

Why the wrong belief is reasonable: the paycheck stops, and for thirty years the advice was "max out the pre-tax 401(k), you'll be in a lower bracket later." For a $1M to $4M pre-tax saver, RMDs (from 75 if born 1960 or later [F-014], sized by the IRS divisor [F-105]), up to 85% of Social Security taxed [F-019], and IRMAA tiers based on income from two years earlier [F-021, F-024] can stack retirement income back up to working-year levels.
