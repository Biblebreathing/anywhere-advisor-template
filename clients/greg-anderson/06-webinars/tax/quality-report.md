## 3. Fact check

- **Fact IDs used in the deck (32):** F-002, F-003, F-005, F-006, F-007, F-011, F-013 to F-025, F-032, F-034, F-035, F-092 to F-096, F-102 to F-106. All exist in the registry.
- **New facts added to `03-deep-research/fact-registry.md` (section K):**
  - **F-102** Roth qualified distributions: 59½ (or other event) + 5-year rule; not included in income; separate 5-year clock per conversion (IRS Pub 590-B, 2025)
  - **F-103** Supplemental wage withholding: flat 22%; 37% above $1M (IRS Pub 15, 2026). Flag: Pub 15 excerpt doesn't name RSUs, so the script says "the IRS lets employers withhold a flat 22% on supplemental pay; check whether that's what you see on your vests"
  - **F-104** Surviving spouse filing status: joint for the year of death; qualifying surviving spouse only with a dependent child, for 2 years; otherwise single (IRS Pub 501, 2025)
  - **F-105** RMD = prior Dec 31 balance ÷ IRS factor; Uniform Lifetime factor at 75 = 24.6 (IRS RMD FAQs; Pub 590-B Table III)
  - **F-106** Medicare eligibility generally at 65; 7-month Initial Enrollment Period (Medicare.gov)
- **Derived arithmetic (all hypothetical, labeled):** $3,000,000 ÷ 24.6 ≈ $121,951 (F-105); 85% × $80,000 = $68,000 taxable SS (F-019); $122K + $68K + $30K ≈ $220K > $218K IRMAA line (F-021); joint vs. single 24% bracket $211,400 vs. $105,700 and IRMAA $218K vs. $109K (F-002, F-003, F-021); born 1963 → 63 in 2026 → Medicare 2028 → RMD 2038 (F-014, F-024, F-106). "Divide by 25" is presented as a rough estimate of the 24.6 factor.
- **Not used (unsourced or unverified):** Colorado 2026 rate (F-031 unverified for 2026); senior-deduction phase-out rate; any "secondary business entity" tax savings (fact sheet §7: needs the EA/CPA's own support); employer names (market brief recommends against).
