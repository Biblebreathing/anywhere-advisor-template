Questions people ask

Is the PATH Review really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation, and you keep your PATH Map either way.

Is this a sales pitch?

No. The hour is about your situation. If we can help, Greg will tell you exactly how. If not, he'll tell you that too.

Who do you work with?

Mostly engineers, technical professionals and long-time employees of industry and government, in their late 50s and 60s, who want a clear income plan. Many also own rentals or a business.

What should I bring?

Last year's tax return, your latest account statements, and your Social Security estimates if you have them. We'll send a short checklist.

Do I have to come to Fort Collins?

No. We meet clients across the country by Zoom.
