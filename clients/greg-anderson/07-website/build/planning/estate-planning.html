Estate Planning Coordination in Fort Collins

A plan isn't about how much you have. It's about who decides when you can't, and whether your family ends up in court.

Reserve My PATH Reviewor join the free class →

Many families think estate planning is about estate tax. In Colorado, for most families, it isn't: Colorado has no estate or inheritance tax, and the federal exemption is very high.

It's about incapacity, court time, and getting money to the right people without a fight. That's the part most people miss.

Does this sound familiar?

  • Your will was signed years ago, or you don't have one
  • You're not sure who your beneficiaries are
  • You have a family member who needs special care or protection
  • You own rentals or property in another state

What every family should know

1. A will doesn't keep you out of probate court

Some assets pass by beneficiary form or title. Others, like a home in your name alone, may go through the court [ATTORNEY REVIEW].

2. Incapacity is the part most people miss

Durable powers of attorney and medical directives let the right people act for you without a court.

3. Beneficiary forms usually win

An old beneficiary form can override what your will says. Check them every time life changes.
