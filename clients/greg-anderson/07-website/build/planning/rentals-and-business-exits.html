Does this sound familiar?

What every family should know

1. Selling rentals to fund your paycheck

A sale, an exchange or an installment sale each change the tax and the income. Plan before you list.

2. A three-to-five-year business exit plan

Succession, marketing the business confidentially, transition risk and the tax impact, planned in advance.

3. From property to paycheck

Turn the sale proceeds into part of your Retirement Paycheck Map.

How we help

Questions people ask

It depends on the tax, the income they produce and how much work they are. We'll run the numbers with you.
