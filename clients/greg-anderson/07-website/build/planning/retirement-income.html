Most people approach retirement asking, "Have we saved enough?" It's a fair question, and it's the wrong one.

The question that matters is, "Where does our paycheck come from every month, for both of us, for as long as we live?"

Does this sound familiar?

  • You don't know how much you can take out each year
  • You haven't decided when each of you will claim Social Security
  • You've never seen your retirement income on one page

What every family should know

1. Your paycheck, not your pile

Start with what you'll spend and the income you can count on. The gap is what your savings must cover.

2. The first years matter most

A market drop right after you retire hurts more than the same drop ten years later, because you're withdrawing at the same time.

3. Plan for two people

Social Security choices and survivor income decide what the surviving spouse lives on.
