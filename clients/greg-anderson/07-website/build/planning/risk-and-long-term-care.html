Investment Risk and Long-Term Care Planning

The stock market helps you build savings. In retirement it can hurt you, if the wrong money is in the wrong place.

Reserve My PATH Review

Most retirement portfolios are still built the way they were at 45: for growth.

Once you're taking income, the money you'll live on in the next few years needs a different job than the money you won't touch for fifteen.

Does this sound familiar?

  • All your money is invested the same way
  • You don't know your risk score
  • You have no plan for long-term care

What every family should know

1. Money by purpose

Income money, growth money and reserve money, each positioned for its job.

2. Know your number

Your risk score shows how much a market drop could move your plan.

3. Long-term care is a plan, not a policy

Home care, assisted living and nursing care can be planned for in several ways, including from your own assets.
