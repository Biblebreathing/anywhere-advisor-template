# Book Blueprint — *A Path to Run On* (Quill Step 8, Deliverable 1)

**Title:** A Path to Run On · **Subtitle:** The PATH Plan for Turning Your Savings into a Retirement Paycheck, Planning Your Taxes, and Protecting Your Family
**Alternates:** A (signature saying, matches the webinar): *The Tax Trap: What Your 401(k) Isn't Telling You, and the PATH Plan for Getting Out* · B (giftable): *Two Plus Two Equals Four: A Plain-Spoken Guide to Retirement Income, Taxes and Family Protection*
**One-sentence promise:** By the last page you'll know where your retirement paycheck comes from, how your money is positioned, when the IRS collects, and how your family is protected, and you'll have the worksheets to prove it.
**Target reader:** Married engineers, technical professionals and long-time employees, 58–68, with $1M–$4M mostly in 401(k)s and IRAs; often RSUs, a rental or a small business. Also their adult children.
**Book model:** C. Mechanism Field Guide · **Length target:** about 30,000 words · introduction, 12 chapters, closing
**Villain:** the Three-Office Problem · **Mechanism:** the PATH Plan (placeholder) · **CTA:** the PATH Review → PATH Map (+ CPA What-If)

**Title check:** Amazon title search and compliance **[TO DO]**. No guarantees or promised outcomes in the title or subtitle.

## Part I: The Three-Office Problem

### Chapter 1: Built to Grow, Not to Pay
- **Hook:** Dave's eleven-tab spreadsheet and the question it can't answer: "How much can we spend next year?"
- **Teaching point:** Accumulation and distribution are different jobs. The same portfolio, the same habits and the same advice don't carry over.
- **Story:** Dave and Linda (composite)
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `paycheck-question` (The Paycheck Question)
- **The One Thing:** Write down the number you think you can spend next year, and where it comes from.
- **QR:** `ch01-paycheck-question` → hub#ch1
- **Facts used:** F-055 life expectancy at 65 (CDC); F-061 Bengen 1994
- **Takeaway:** The problem isn't the size of the pile.

### Chapter 2: Three Offices, No Table
- **Hook:** Three phone calls on the same Tuesday: the CPA, the investment rep, the attorney. None of them knows what the other said.
- **Teaching point:** The Three-Office Problem: tax, legal and investment decisions interact; made separately, they collide.
- **Story:** Greg's "no logo on my business card" decision; a composite collision (conversion vs. IRMAA vs. trust titling)
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `who-is-at-my-table` (Who's at My Table?)
- **The One Thing:** List your advisor, CPA and attorney, and the last time any two of them spoke.
- **QR:** `ch02-who-is-at-my-table` → hub#ch2
- **Facts used:** F-024 IRMAA look-back
- **Takeaway:** The gaps live between the offices.

### Chapter 3: Head Trash
- **Hook:** Linda awake at 2 a.m. doing math she's not good at.
- **Teaching point:** The excuses good savers use ("I'm an engineer," "my CPA handles it," "later") and what's underneath them. The true belief: a plan you can explain to your spouse beats a perfect spreadsheet.
- **Story:** [GAP: a resistant engineer who changed his mind; a family that waited]
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `excuse-inventory` (My Excuse Inventory)
- **The One Thing:** Circle the excuse you've used most, and write what it's costing you.
- **QR:** `assessment` → assessment
- **Facts used:** F-065 to F-069 EBRI 2026 Retirement Confidence Survey
- **Takeaway:** Take your PATH Score.

## Part II: The PATH Plan

### Chapter 4: P Is for Paycheck
- **Hook:** "What's our paycheck the year after you retire?" The silence that follows.
- **Teaching point:** Build the paycheck from the spending side first: essential vs. lifestyle spending, then dependable income, then the gap.
- **Story:** [GAP: a client whose paycheck map changed the retirement date]
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `retirement-paycheck-map` (My Retirement Paycheck Map)
- **The One Thing:** Fill in your Paycheck Map, even roughly.
- **QR:** `ch04-retirement-paycheck-map` → hub#ch4
- **Facts used:** Spending and income figures from the registry
- **Takeaway:** You need a paycheck, not a pile.

### Chapter 5: Social Security Is a Two-Person Decision
- **Hook:** The widow's check: what the survivor actually lives on.
- **Teaching point:** Claiming age, the survivor benefit and the earnings test; why the higher earner's decision is really the survivor's decision.
- **Story:** Greg as a Social Security teacher; [GAP: a survivor story]
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `social-security-decision` (Our Social Security Decision)
- **The One Thing:** Get both of your Social Security estimates this week.
- **QR:** `ch05-social-security-decision` → hub#ch5
- **Facts used:** SSA FRA, reductions, credits, COLA, earnings test (registry)
- **Takeaway:** Decide for two.

### Chapter 6: A Is for Assets: The First Years Matter Most
- **Hook:** A couple in 2021, thirty years of savings still invested for growth.
- **Teaching point:** Sequence-of-returns risk; money by purpose (income, growth, reserve); the risk score.
- **Story:** Story 1 (consent + compliance; use F-062 -19.44% / F-063 -25.4%)
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `money-by-purpose` (My Money by Purpose)
- **The One Thing:** Sort your accounts into income, growth and reserve money.
- **QR:** `assessment` → assessment
- **Facts used:** F-062, F-063, F-061
- **Takeaway:** Give every dollar a job.

### Chapter 7: The Care Question
- **Hook:** The phone call about a parent's fall.
- **Teaching point:** Long-term care as a plan, not a product: costs in Colorado, Medicaid's look-back, ways to pay including your own assets.
- **Story:** [GAP: an LTC story]
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `care-plan` (Our Care Plan)
- **The One Thing:** Talk with your spouse about where you'd want care, and how you'd pay for it.
- **QR:** `ch07-care-plan` → hub#ch7
- **Facts used:** CareScout 2025 Colorado costs; Medicaid 60-month look-back; 2026 CMS limits
- **Takeaway:** Plan the care, then choose the tools.

### Chapter 8: T Is for Taxes: The Tax Trap
- **Hook:** Greg at graduation: a tax law turns an industry into "a ghost town."
- **Teaching point:** Part of the 401(k) belongs to the IRS; RMDs at 73 or 75; Social Security taxation; Medicare's two-year look-back; the years between retirement and RMDs.
- **Story:** Greg's career pivot (F-092 to F-094)
- **Exercise / worksheet:** `tax-trap-check` (My Tax Trap Check)

- **QR:** `webinar` → webinar
- **Facts used:** F-013, F-014, F-021, F-024, 2026 brackets
- **Takeaway:** Plan when the IRS collects.

### Chapter 9: Your Tax Return Is the Map

### Chapter 10: H Is for Heirs: One Year and One Day
- **Hook:** A couple signs their trust and powers of attorney. One year and one day later...

- **Story:** Story 2 (consent: widow) [ATTORNEY REVIEW throughout]
