# Compliance Checklist (run on every public asset)

Not legal advice. The advisor's compliance department, broker-dealer, or IMO has final say. Each asset must be submitted for approval before it goes live.

- [ ] **Which rules apply:** FINRA Rule 2210 (BD reps), SEC Marketing Rule 206(4)-1 (RIAs), state insurance advertising rules (insurance products). Hybrid advisors may need all three.
- [ ] No "guaranteed", "risk-free", "safe", or "no loss" language unless the product contract supports it, with the required qualifiers (e.g., guarantees based on claims-paying ability of the issuer).
- [ ] Tax and legal statements carry an "not tax/legal advice; consult your professional" disclosure. Estate topics point to an attorney.
- [ ] No performance figures, projections, or hypotheticals without the required disclosures and approval.
- [ ] Testimonials/endorsements only if the firm permits them, with required disclosures (paid/unpaid, client/non-client).
- [ ] Webinar/seminar invitations clearly disclose that insurance or investment products may be discussed (many states require this for "educational" events).
- [ ] Registration and firm disclosure in the footer of every page, email, and the book's copyright page.
- [ ] The book is treated as a marketing communication: submit the full manuscript for approval.
- [ ] Assessments/calculators: inputs and assumptions are disclosed, and results are labeled educational, not a recommendation.
- [ ] Every number and statistic cites a current fact in `03-deep-research/fact-registry.md`. State legal facts marked `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` have been confirmed by the advisor's attorney.
- [ ] Process and program names, titles, and the appointment offer contain no implied guarantees or outcome promises, and no unaccredited "senior" or "specialist" titles.
- [ ] Scarcity ("limited seats," "only X sessions a month") and deadlines are true.
- [ ] Data capture: privacy policy linked, SMS consent language (TCPA) for text follow-up.
