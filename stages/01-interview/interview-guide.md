# Advisor Interview Guide

~60–90 minutes. Record it. Ask for **stories and examples**, not just answers. Stories turn into webinar hooks, book chapters, and ad copy.
Questions marked ★ are the most important if you're short on time.

---

## Part 1 — Identity & Credentials (5 min)
1. ★ Full name as it should appear publicly, firm name, city/region served.
2. Licenses and registrations (Series 6/7/63/65/66, life & health, CFP®, ChFC, CLU, RICP, etc.). Are you with a **broker-dealer, an RIA, insurance-only, or hybrid**? *(This decides which compliance rules apply.)*
3. Who must approve your marketing (compliance department, BD, IMO/FMO)? How long does approval usually take?
4. Years in the business, assets under management / premium placed (if they can share it publicly), number of client households.

## Part 2 — Origin Story (10 min)
5. ★ Why did you become an advisor? Was there a moment, such as a family event, a loss, or a mentor?
6. What did you see go wrong for families that made you want to do this work differently?
7. What do you believe that most advisors don't? *(A contrarian belief becomes the "big idea.")*

## Part 3 — Ideal Client & Market (15 min)
8. ★ Describe your best 3 clients (no names): age, occupation, assets, family situation, what they were worried about.
9. ★ What is the #1 fear or frustration they bring to the first meeting? What words do they use?
10. What do they think the problem is, versus what the problem actually is?
11. Who is **not** a fit? (minimum assets, situations you avoid)
12. Any niche: business owners, teachers/public employees, retirees from a specific employer, farmers, medical professionals, a faith community, a specific ethnic or language community?
13. Describe your local market: demographics, big employers, what's changing (retirements, pension changes, property values).

## Part 4 — Services & Planning Focus (10 min)
14. ★ Which planning areas do you actually want more of? Rank them: **Estate · Tax · Retirement Income · Asset Protection · Long-Term Care · Investments · Life Insurance.**
15. What products or strategies do you use most (annuities, IUL, managed accounts, Roth conversions, trusts with partner attorneys, hybrid LTC)?
16. How are you paid (fees, commissions, both)? Average client value in year one, and lifetime?
17. Do you partner with attorneys, CPAs, or others? Could they co-host webinars?

## Part 5 — Process & Unique Mechanism (15 min)
18. ★ Walk me through what happens from first contact to signed client, step by step.
19. ★ Do you have a named process, framework, or plan? If not, what are the 3–5 steps you always follow? *(This becomes the Unique Mechanism.)*
20. What do you deliver in the first meeting that surprises people?
21. Any tools you already use: risk questionnaires, income projections, tax maps, reports?
22. What's your close rate from first meeting? Where do people drop off?

## Part 6 — Proof & Stories (10 min)
23. ★ Tell me 3 client success stories: the situation before, what you did, the result after. *(Anonymized. These become case studies.)*
24. A story about a family who **didn't** plan and what it cost them.
25. Media, awards, speaking, books, or articles you've done already.
26. Do you have reviews or testimonials? Are you allowed to use them under your firm's rules?

## Part 7 — Marketing Today (5 min)
27. ★ Where do new clients come from now (referrals, seminars, mailers, digital ads, COIs)? How many new appointments per month?
28. Have you run seminars or webinars before? What worked and what didn't? Dinner seminars?
29. Monthly marketing budget. Goal: how many new appointments per month and new clients per year?
30. Calendar link / scheduling tool, CRM, email platform, website URL, social accounts.

## Part 8 — The Book (10 min)
31. ★ If you wrote one book that every prospect read before meeting you, what would it convince them of?
32. Working title ideas? Tone: warm and story-driven, or direct and technical?
33. What 3 mistakes do you most want readers to avoid?
34. What worksheets or exercises would help a reader get ready for a meeting with you?
35. Comfortable on video? Willing to record a short welcome video for the book's QR codes?

## Part 9 — Voice & Brand (5 min)
36. Three words clients use to describe you.
37. Brand colors, logo, headshots available?
38. Phrases you **never** want used (e.g., "guaranteed", "risk-free", "Wall Street sharks").

---

### Closing
"What did I not ask that I should have?" Then confirm the next step and the date for the knowledge-base review.
