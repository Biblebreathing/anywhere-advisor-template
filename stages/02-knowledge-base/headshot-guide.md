# Getting a Great Headshot (one page, for advisors)

Your headshot goes on your website, your book cover, your webinar slides, and every email. It's the first thing prospects see, so it's worth getting right.

**Best option:** a professional photographer (often $150–$400). Ask for **high-resolution files and permission for commercial use** (website, book, ads).

**If you're using a phone:**
- Use the back camera, not the selfie camera. Have someone else take it at chest height.
- Face a window: soft daylight on your face, no harsh overhead lights, no direct sun.
- Use a plain, uncluttered background, or stand 6+ feet in front of greenery or a bookshelf so it blurs.
- Wear solid colors (navy, gray, and earth tones work well). Avoid busy patterns and logos.
- Frame from mid-chest up, with room above your head.
- Take 30+ shots and vary your expression: a warm smile with teeth, a soft smile, and a few "talking" shots.

**Send us:** 3–5 favorites, the original full-size files (not screenshots or social media downloads), and one relaxed lifestyle photo if you have it.
