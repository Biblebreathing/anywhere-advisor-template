# Knowledge Base Review Checklist

Compare the draft `knowledge-base.md` against `transcript.md`. Fix anything the transcript can answer. Put everything else on the follow-up list.

## Accuracy
- [ ] Every quote in quotation marks appears **word for word** in the transcript. Search for each one. Fix any paraphrases.
- [ ] No story, number, credential, or client detail appears that the advisor didn't say. Remove anything that isn't in the transcript, or mark it `[INFERRED]`.
- [ ] Facts match the intake form (name, firm, registration type, licenses). Where they conflict, flag it.

## Completeness
- [ ] Every ★ question was asked and answered. List any that weren't on the follow-up list.
- [ ] **Story Bank:** every story the advisor told is captured, including those mentioned in passing. Read the transcript for them; interview AIs often drop the minor ones.
- [ ] **Anchor story** (section 2) has enough detail to open a book chapter: who, what was missing, cost, how it felt.
- [ ] **Process** (section 5) has distinct steps in the advisor's words, enough to name a Unique Mechanism in Stage 04.
- [ ] **Book foundation** has the thesis, the mistakes, and the one action.
- [ ] **Voice profile** is specific to this person. "Professional and friendly" doesn't count.
- [ ] Section 7 has at least 5 strong quotable lines. If not, find more in the transcript.

## Flags
- [ ] `[VERIFY]` on every number, statistic, dollar amount, and legal, tax, or product claim.
- [ ] `[COMPLIANCE]` on every loose regulated term: *fiduciary, guaranteed, safe, risk-free, tax-free, no fees, protected*, returns or performance, and testimonials. Check these against the registration type (an insurance-only agent calling themselves a fiduciary is a serious problem).
- [ ] `[ANONYMIZE]` on every real person named, and every sensitive detail (health, divorce, money trouble, addiction, a death) flagged for consent, **including the advisor's own family members.**

## Length & usability
- [ ] 2,500–3,500 words. If it's longer, cut repetition first, then lower-value stories.
- [ ] A writer who has never met the advisor could start a webinar or a book chapter from it.

## Output of this review
- The corrected draft (overwrite `knowledge-base.md`, header status: **In Review**)
- The follow-up list: at most 10 questions, most important first, plus consent items
