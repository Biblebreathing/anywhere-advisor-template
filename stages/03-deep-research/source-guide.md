# Source Guide

Use **primary sources** whenever they exist. Check the publication date; for any figure marked 🔁, the source must be for the current year.

## Federal figures (🔁 annual)
| Topic | Primary source |
|---|---|
| Tax brackets, standard deduction, estate & gift exemption, annual gift exclusion, IRA/401(k) limits, RMD rules | IRS (irs.gov): annual inflation-adjustment Revenue Procedure, "Retirement topics" pages, newsroom releases |
| Social Security: COLA, full retirement age, earnings test, taxation of benefits | Social Security Administration (ssa.gov) |
| Medicare Part B/D premiums, IRMAA brackets, coverage rules | CMS (cms.gov), Medicare (medicare.gov) |
| HSA limits | IRS |
| Federal law changes | Congress.gov (bill text and summaries), IRS guidance |

## State rules
| Topic | Primary source |
|---|---|
| State income tax on retirement income | The state's department of revenue; Tax Foundation (taxfoundation.org) as a secondary cross-check |
| Estate / inheritance tax | The state's department of revenue |
| Probate, trusts, small-estate limits, homestead | The state's statutes (the legislature's website) and state court self-help pages · `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` |
| Medicaid long-term care eligibility, look-back, estate recovery | The state's Medicaid agency; Medicaid.gov · `[ATTORNEY REVIEW]` |
| Insurance advertising rules | The state department of insurance; NAIC (naic.org) model rules |

## Market and demographic data
| Topic | Source |
|---|---|
| Population by age, income, home values, homeownership | U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (data.census.gov) |
| Employers, industries, wages | Bureau of Labor Statistics (bls.gov); state labor department |
| Household wealth and retirement savings | Federal Reserve Survey of Consumer Finances (federalreserve.gov) |
| Long-term care costs by state and metro area | CareScout Cost of Care Survey (formerly Genworth) |
| Long-term care need, likelihood, duration | ACL / LongTermCare.gov (HHS) |

## Consumer attitudes (headline statistics)
| Topic | Source |
|---|---|
| Retirement confidence and worries | EBRI Retirement Confidence Survey (ebri.org) |
| Annuity and retirement-income research | LIMRA; Alliance for Lifetime Income |
| Wills and estate-planning rates | Caring.com annual wills survey; Gallup |
| Older-adult finances | AARP Public Policy Institute; CFPB (consumerfinance.gov) |

## Avoid
- Blog posts, product-vendor marketing pages, and AI summaries **as the cited source.** You can use them to find a primary source, then cite that.
- Statistics with no traceable original study ("studies show 70%…").
- Out-of-date figures. If the current year's figure isn't published yet, use last year's, and state its year.

## Compliance reminder
Registration details of competitors can be checked on FINRA BrokerCheck (brokercheck.finra.org) and SEC IAPD (adviserinfo.sec.gov). Use them for research only. Never disparage competitors in public materials.
