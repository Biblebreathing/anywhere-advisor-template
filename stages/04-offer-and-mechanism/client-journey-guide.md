# Client Journey Guide

The client journey maps every step from **stranger → client → advocate**: what the person experiences, which asset moves them forward, what's automated, and what we measure. It's the blueprint that Stages 05–10 build, and the dashboard the advisor manages by.

**The North Star metric is booked, *kept* appointments with qualified prospects.** Every stage exists to raise the number of people who reach the appointment, show up, and become clients.

---

## The default Anywhere Advisor journey

Adapt it per advisor. Not every advisor uses every entry point.

| # | Stage | The prospect… | Assets & touchpoints | Automation | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Attract** | Sees an ad, the book on Amazon, a referral, a partner's invitation, a post | Ads, book listing, content, co-hosted events with CPAs or attorneys | Ad platform → landing page | Cost per lead |
| 2 | **Capture** | Takes an assessment, requests the book, or registers for a webinar | Assessment (05), book offer page (10), webinar registration (06/08) | Contact → CRM, with tags for source and track | Opt-in rate |
| 3 | **Show up** | Gets reminders, assessment results, and the book | Results page + email, reminder sequence, book delivery sequence | Email + SMS reminders (with consent) | Webinar attendance rate |
| 4 | **Learn** | Attends the webinar, reads the book, scans a QR code | Webinar (teaches one mechanism step), book chapters, QR → bonus hub | Behavior tags: attended, watched to the offer, scanned | Watched-to-offer rate |
| 5 | **Book** | Books the named appointment | The appointment offer (see `offer-stack-template.md`), calendar | Calendar → CRM, pre-meeting questionnaire | Appointment booking rate |
| 6 | **Keep** | Confirms and prepares | Confirmation, a short video from the advisor, "what to bring" checklist, reminders 48h / 24h / 2h | Email + SMS sequence | **Show rate** |
| 7 | **Meeting 1: Discovery** | Talks through goals and worries; walks the mechanism | Meeting agenda = mechanism steps; their assessment results | Notes → CRM | Meeting 1 → Meeting 2 rate |
| 8 | **Meeting 2: Plan** | Sees their plan on the mechanism diagram, with deliverables | Named deliverables (Paycheck Map, Tax Timeline…) | Task reminders for the advisor | Close rate |
| 9 | **Meeting 3: Implement** | Signs and starts | Paperwork checklist | Onboarding sequence triggers | Time to implement |
| 10 | **Onboard** | Feels welcomed and confident | Welcome kit (signed book, mechanism one-pager), "what happens next" | Welcome email series | 90-day satisfaction |
| 11 | **Review** | Annual or semi-annual review | Review agenda walks the mechanism steps again; cross-sell remaining steps | Review scheduling automation | Retention rate, additional assets or policies |
| 12 | **Advocate** | Brings friends and family | "Bring a friend" webinars, client appreciation events, extra copies of the book to share | Referral request at points of delight | Referrals per client per year |

### The re-engagement loop (don't skip this)
Most prospects won't book the first time. Non-bookers enter a **long-term nurture**:
- Invite them to the **next webinar track** (different topic, same mechanism, same appointment)
- Send a monthly educational email in the advisor's voice
- Offer an assessment re-take after six months, or when a trigger event is likely (birthday milestones like age 59½, 62, 65, and 73; tax season; open enrollment)
- Tag by interest (which track they attended) so invitations are relevant

### Meeting sequence
The mechanism becomes the agenda:
- **Meeting 1 (Discovery):** confirm the trigger and the fear, walk the mechanism, find where they stand on each step (using their assessment results), and agree on which steps matter most. Close: set Meeting 2.
- **Meeting 2 (Plan):** present the plan step by step, with each deliverable. Recommendations with disclosures. Close: decide.
- **Meeting 3 (Implementation):** paperwork, timelines, and what happens next. Onboarding begins.

Adjust to the advisor's real process from Knowledge Base section 5. Some close in two meetings.

## Targets: starting assumptions, not facts

Use these only until the advisor has their own data. Mark them `[ASSUMPTION]` and replace them with actual numbers after 60–90 days.

| Metric | Starting target |
|---|---|
| Webinar registration → live attendance | 30–40% (higher with SMS reminders) |
| Attendee → booked appointment | 10–20% |
| Booked → kept appointment (show rate) | 75–85% with a full reminder sequence |
| Meeting 1 → client | 30–50% |
| Assessment start → completion | 50–70% |
| Assessment completion → booked | 5–15% |

Work backward from the advisor's goal (intake: new appointments per month) to the number of leads and registrants needed. Put that math in `client-journey.md`.

## Roles: who does what
For each touchpoint, mark who handles it: **AI/automation**, **staff**, or **advisor**. The goal of the Anywhere Advisor model is that the advisor spends their time in Meetings 1–3 and on reviews, and almost everything else is automated or delegated.
