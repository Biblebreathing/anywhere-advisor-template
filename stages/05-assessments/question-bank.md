# Question Bank

Starting points, organized by the kind of mechanism step. **Rewrite them in the advisor's voice and terms**, match them to the advisor's actual steps, and keep 2–3 per step. Scores are shown as (3 / 1 / 0).

## Income / retirement paycheck
- Do you know how much monthly income you'll need in retirement? *(Written budget / Rough idea / Not really)*
- Do you know how much will come from Social Security, pensions, or other dependable sources? *(Yes / Roughly / No)*
- Have you decided when you (and your spouse) will claim Social Security, and why? *(Yes, with analysis / I have a target age / Not yet)*
- Do you have a plan for which accounts to draw from first, and how much each year? *(Written / Thought about it / No)*
- If you retired today, do you know how long your savings would last at your planned spending? *(Yes / Roughly / No idea)*

## Tax
- Do you know how much of your savings is pre-tax vs. Roth vs. taxable? *(Yes / Somewhat / No)*
- Have you looked at how required minimum distributions could affect your taxes later? *(With a professional / On my own / Not yet)*
- Has anyone reviewed whether Roth conversions or a different withdrawal order could lower your lifetime taxes? *(In the last 2 years / Years ago / Never)*
- Do you know how your retirement income could affect Medicare premiums or the taxes on your Social Security? *(Yes / Heard of it / No)*

## Protection: market and income risk
- If the market dropped 25% early in retirement, do you know how it would affect your income? *(Have a plan / Probably okay / Don't know)*
- How much of your essential monthly expenses would be covered by income that doesn't depend on the market? *(All / Some / Not sure)*
- If you passed away first, does your spouse know what income they would have? *(Planned / Somewhat / No)*

## Protection: long-term care
- Do you have a plan to pay for long-term care if you or your spouse need it? *(In place / Looked into it / No)*
- Have you and your family talked about where and how you'd want to receive care? *(Yes / Briefly / No)*
- Do you know what home care or a nursing home costs in your area? *(Yes / Roughly / No)*

## Protection: asset protection
- Have you reviewed how your savings and home would be affected by a lawsuit, a long-term care need, or a family member's divorce or debts? *(Yes / Partly / No)*
- Are your largest assets titled and protected the way you intend? *(Reviewed recently / Not sure / Never reviewed)*
- Do you have enough liability coverage (for example, an umbrella policy) for your net worth? *(Yes / Not sure / No)*

## Legacy / estate
- Do you have an up-to-date will or trust (reviewed in the last 5 years)? *(Yes / Older / No)*
- If you have a trust, have your accounts and property been retitled into it? *(Yes / Some / No, or not sure)*
- Are the beneficiaries on your retirement accounts and life insurance current? *(Checked recently / Think so / Not sure)*
- Have you named someone to make financial and medical decisions if you can't? *(Both / One / Neither)*
- Would your family know where to find your documents, accounts, and passwords? *(Yes / Mostly / No)*
- Have you talked with your children or heirs about your wishes? *(Yes / Some / No)*

## Review / organization
- How often do you review your whole retirement plan, not just investments? *(Yearly / Every few years / Rarely)*
- Do your advisor, CPA, and attorney coordinate with each other? *(Yes / Sometimes / No, or I don't have all three)*
- Are your financial documents in one organized place? *(Yes / Mostly / No)*

## Qualifiers (unscored)
- When do you plan to retire (or did you)? *(Already retired · Within 5 years · 5–10 years · 10+ years)*
- Roughly how much have you saved for retirement, not counting your home? *(Ranges set to the advisor's minimum · Prefer not to say)*
- Are you married or planning with a partner? *(Yes · No)*, useful for survivor and estate messaging
- What state do you live in? Use this only if the advisor is licensed in limited states; mark unlicensed states as not qualified, and route them to the webinar or book.
