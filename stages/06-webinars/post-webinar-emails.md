# Post-Webinar Emails — {{Webinar Title}}

Short, plain, in the advisor's voice, signed by the advisor. Stage 08 loads these into the CRM, with SMS versions for contacts who gave consent. Stop the sequence as soon as someone books.

## Attended (did not book)
**1. Within 1 hour: "Thank you, plus the replay and your workbook"**
Thanks; the one idea to remember (the true belief); the replay link (available for {{48–72}} hours); the workbook; one line on the {{Named Appointment}} with a booking link.

**2. Day 1: "The question I got most"**
Answer the most common question from the live chat (or a seeded one). Close: "If you'd like to look at your own situation, here's my calendar."

**3. Day 3: "A story"**
A short client story (anonymized, with consent) that illustrates the Big Idea, plus the deliverable they'd walk away with. Booking link.

**4. Day 5: "Last call on the replay, and my calendar this month"**
The replay is coming down; honest capacity ("I have {{N}} Blueprint Sessions left this month"). Booking link. Then the contact moves into the re-engagement loop (next track invitation, monthly email).

## Registered, did not attend
**1. Within 1 hour: "Sorry we missed you. Here's the replay"**
The replay link plus the workbook; the one-sentence promise of the webinar.

**2. Day 1: "The 3 things I covered"**
The three promises as bullets, each with one sentence of substance; the replay link.

**3. Day 3: "Replay comes down soon"**
Replay deadline; booking link for anyone who'd rather just talk. Then the contact gets an invitation to the next live session (same or next track).

## Booked
The confirmation and pre-meeting sequence is built in Stage 08 (show-rate sequence).

**Compliance:** no new claims beyond the approved webinar; disclosure in every email footer; an opt-out link in every email; SMS only with consent.
