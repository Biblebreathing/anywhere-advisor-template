# How to Use the Track Templates

Each track template supplies the **topic-specific raw material** for one of the five Anywhere Advisor webinars. The **structure** always comes from `../webinar-framework.md` (3 acts, about 45 slides). The **advisor-specific content** always comes from their Knowledge Base, research, and Stage 04 files.

| Track | Template | Typical mechanism step |
|---|---|---|
| Estate Planning | `estate-planning.md` | Legacy |
| Tax | `tax.md` | Tax |
| Retirement Paycheck | `retirement-paycheck.md` | Income |
| Asset Protection | `asset-protection.md` | Protection |
| Long-Term Care | `long-term-care.md` | Protection |

**Rules**
- Everything here is a **default to customize.** Replace it with the advisor's own words, stories, and process wherever they exist.
- **Numbers:** templates list *which* facts to use, never the figures themselves. Every figure comes from the client's `03-deep-research/fact-registry.md` with its fact ID.
- **Stories:** only from the Knowledge Base story bank and with consent logged. If a slot has no real story, use a teaching example clearly framed as hypothetical ("Imagine a couple who…"), never as a real client.
- **Titles:** test 2–3 options. The title is 80% of registration (400-40).
