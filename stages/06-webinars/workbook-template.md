# Webinar Workbook Template — {{Webinar Title}}

4–6 pages, branded (Stage 02 colors and fonts), sent with the registration confirmation and the day-of reminder. Each page matches a `workbook_cue` in the script. Leave space to write.

**Cover:** webinar title · advisor name and photo · "Have this open during the class"

**Page 1: Where I am today.** 3–5 quick fill-ins tied to Big Idea 1 (e.g., "My monthly income needs: $____")

**Page 2: Big Idea 1** · the key idea in one sentence, plus a fill-in or checklist

**Page 3: Big Idea 2** · same format (often the risk or "threat" self-score)

**Page 4: Big Idea 3** · same format (the options or strategies to discuss)

**Page 5: My [Mechanism] Readiness Check** · 8–10 yes/no questions across the mechanism steps (mirror the assessment from Stage 05). "Count your NO answers."

**Page 6: My next step** · "Questions I want to ask in my [Named Appointment]" (5 blank lines) · booking link plus QR code · the advisor's disclosure

Rules: no product names; educational wording; the disclosure in the footer of every page.
