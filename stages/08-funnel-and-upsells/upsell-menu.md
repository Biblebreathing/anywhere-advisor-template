# Upsell Menu

In an advisor funnel, the "upsell" is the **next, bigger yes**, offered at the natural moment. Pick the ones that fit the advisor's model; never let an upsell distract from the main goal: **a kept appointment with a qualified prospect.**

## Free upsells (use these with every advisor)
| Upsell | Where | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **"Can't wait? Book your session now"** | Thank-you page after registration; assessment results | Some registrants are ready today. Let them skip the line. |
| **Take the assessment** | Thank-you page; S1 emails | A micro-commitment that raises attendance and gives the advisor data before the meeting |
| **Bring your spouse or partner** | S1 24-hour reminder; S2 confirmation | Couples decide together. A meeting with both is far more likely to move forward. |
| **The workbook** | Thank-you page; reminders | People who prepare show up and engage |
| **Register for the next track** | Post-webinar emails; nurture | Same mechanism, new topic, another chance to book |
| **The book** | Website, nurture, client onboarding | Authority and a referral tool (Stage 09–10) |
| **"Bring a friend" webinar invite** | Client onboarding (S9); after a kept meeting | Referrals from the warmest source |

## Paid upsells (optional; only if they fit the advisor and compliance approves)
| Offer | Price range | Use when | Cautions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Printed book + workbook bundle** | Cost of shipping to ~$25 | The book exists (Stage 09) | Educational only |
| **Self-guided course or "organizer" kit** (e.g., Family Legacy Organizer, Retirement Paycheck Workbook) | $27–$97 | The advisor wants to monetize ad traffic, or filter for buyers | No individualized advice; clear refund policy |
| **Paid planning session / financial plan** | Per the advisor's fee schedule | Fee-based planners (RIAs) | Must match Form ADV and the advisory agreement; disclosed fees |
| **Premium / continuity program** (e.g., annual family meeting, document vault, priority reviews) | Per advisor | Clients, after implementation | Part of the client service model; disclosures as required |

## Timing rules
- **Never upsell before the main conversion** (don't sell a $47 course on the registration page).
- **One upsell per page.**
- **No upsells inside the show-rate sequence (S2).** Its only job is getting them to the meeting.
- Insurance anti-inducement rules: no gifts or bonuses conditioned on buying an insurance product. Keep free bonuses modest and educational.
