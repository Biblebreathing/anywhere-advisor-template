# Book Funnel Guide

A book only builds a practice if readers come back to the advisor. This stage builds the three places a reader lands (the **book offer page**, the **reader bonus hub**, and the **printable worksheets**), the **reader sequence** that follows up, and the **playbook** for using the book to win appointments.

```
Ads · website · webinar · referral partners · mailers
   → BOOK OFFER PAGE (free signed copy / download / Amazon)
        → Reader sequence (S10) → assessment · webinar · named appointment

Printed book (every chapter's QR code: advisor.com/go/chN)
   → READER BONUS HUB (worksheets, videos, chapter anchors)
        → one-time reader sign-up → S10 → appointment
```

---

## 1. The book offer page: pick one offer

| Offer | How it works | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free signed copy, mailed** | The prospect gives name, email, phone (optional), and **mailing address**; the advisor mails a signed copy with a handwritten note and a booking card | **Local advisors.** The address confirms a real local prospect, and a physical book on the kitchen table is a lasting reminder. | Set a geographic limit (licensed states, or a service radius). Budget for the cost of printing and postage. |
| **Free + shipping** | A small shipping charge filters for buyers | National audiences, larger ad budgets | Needs a checkout (Stage 08 paid-upsell rules apply) |
| **Free digital download** | Email → PDF or ebook link | Fast list building, webinar bonuses | Lower perceived value; pair with the signed-copy option for qualified locals |
| **Buy on Amazon** | A link to the listing | Authority and reviews | No lead captured; use alongside one of the above |

**Page structure:** cover image (3D mockup) → headline (the book's promise) → who it's for → 5–7 "inside you'll discover" bullets (by chapter) → the author with photo and credibility → the offer form → FAQ → disclosure. One call to action.

## 2. The reader bonus hub (the QR code target)
- **One section per chapter** with an anchor (`#ch1`, `#ch2`…) so each chapter's QR code lands on the right worksheet. The build tool checks that every QR destination's anchor exists.
- Each section: the chapter title, the worksheet (printable page), an optional short video from the advisor, and the next step (assessment section, webinar, or appointment).
- **One-time reader sign-up** (first name, email, optional mobile with SMS consent) unlocks the downloads. It's remembered on that device, so later scans go straight to the content. It can be turned off (`gate: false`) where compliance or the advisor prefers fully open resources.
- A persistent "Book your [Named Appointment]" bar. Readers are the warmest leads in the system.

## 3. Printable worksheets
Every `worksheets/*.md` from Stage 09 becomes a branded, print-friendly page with a **Print / Save as PDF** button, large write-in spaces, and the advisor's disclosure in the footer.

## 4. Reader sequence (S10)
*Trigger: book requested, or reader sign-up on the hub. Stops when they book.*

| When | Message |
|---|---|
| Immediately | Delivery: the download link, or "your signed copy ships this week," plus the bonus hub link. "Start with Chapter 1. It takes 20 minutes." |
| Day 2 | Why I wrote this book (the anchor story in 150 words). Hub link. |
| Day 5 | "The worksheet most readers skip, and shouldn't": the Chapter [key] worksheet + why. |
| Day 8 | Take the assessment: "See where you stand on all [N] steps of the [Mechanism]." |
| Day 12 | Invitation to the next webinar (matching track) |
| Day 16 | The named appointment: "Bring your book and your worksheets. We'll turn them into your [Deliverable]." |
| Day 21 | **Review request:** "If the book helped, would you leave an honest review on Amazon?" Link. **No incentives for reviews** (against Amazon policy). |
| Day 30 | Pass it on: "Who else in your family should read this? Reply with their address and I'll mail them a copy." (Within the firm's referral rules.) |
| Then | → S8 long-term nurture |

## 5. The book as an appointment engine (playbook)
The book is the advisor's most versatile tool. Use it in every part of the client journey:
1. **Before Meeting 1:** "I'll mail you a copy of my book before we meet." This raises show rates and sends prospects in pre-educated and pre-sold on the mechanism.
2. **At webinars and seminars:** a free signed copy for attendees who book (a modest, educational gift).
3. **Referral partners:** a box of books for CPAs and estate attorneys to give their clients, with a partner-specific QR code (`go/cpa-smith`) to track referrals.
4. **Mail campaigns:** a lumpy-mail package to targeted local households (a book + letter + booking card), where permitted.
5. **Client appreciation:** extra copies for clients to give to friends and family; a signed copy in every welcome kit.
6. **Media and speaking:** "author of" opens doors to local radio, podcasts, libraries, community centers, and employer lunch-and-learns.
7. **The front desk:** every visitor leaves with one.

Track it: a source tag `src:book-<channel>` on every lead (Stage 08 automation map), so the advisor knows which uses produce appointments.

## 6. Launch plan (with Stage 11)
- **2 weeks before:** bonus hub, redirects, and the reader sequence live; QR codes tested from the proof copy; advance copies to 10–20 clients and friends willing to review honestly (they must disclose that they received a free copy).
- **Launch week:** announce to the full list and on social media; the ebook at a launch price (optional); an author webinar ("the 3 big ideas from my new book").
- **Ongoing:** a monthly book-giveaway offer in the nurture email; a book offer on the website home page; the book in the webinar invitation.

## 7. Compliance
- The landing page, hub, worksheets, and emails are marketing communications: approval before launch.
- A free book is an educational gift, not conditioned on any purchase (keep it that way for insurance anti-inducement rules).
- Reviews: no incentives; advance-copy reviewers disclose the free copy. Testimonial rules apply if reviews are reused in advisor marketing.
- SMS only with consent; the privacy policy is linked on every form.
